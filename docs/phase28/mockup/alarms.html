HomePod Alarms on the Timers Page

Under Running, an Alarms section for every alarm set on a HomePod, on or off. Each says its time, its label, the HomePod and room, and when it repeats; a switch turns it on or off, and Delete asks first. Mantel can't stop a ringing alarm (the Home app doesn't show one), so none of these has a Stop. Pick one.

A · A list, like the Clock app (recommended)

One glass plate, a row per alarm: the time large and light, a line under it, the iOS switch at the right. An off alarm goes gray. Edit beside the heading shows a red minus on each row; tapping it asks before deleting. The quietest of the three, and it holds many alarms.

B · Cards, like the running timers

The 388 px timer card: the HomePod in its well (lit while on), the label and where, the time, and two keys: On / Off and Delete. Matches the timers above it; takes more room (three across at 1,280).

C · Pills, like accessories

A 196 x 70 pill each: the time as its name, the room and repeat as its state. A tap turns it on or off (lit while on); press and hold opens a sheet with Delete. The most compact; the least like the Clock app.
