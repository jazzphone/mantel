# Routes

Generated by `tools/route_table.py --write` from each handler's `@guard` or `@open_route` (`hosts/server/mantel_server/route_table.py`); don't edit it by hand. Mantel refuses to start when a route is missing from it or breaks its rules ([Rules](Rules.md) rule 7).

391 routes in 69 modules, under every combination of the options that add routes.

Columns: **Rule** who may call it (`signed_in` any session, `person`, `manager` an Owner or Admin, `owner`; whether a wall tablet's session may; the door; how fresh the sign-in must be) or, for a route with its own check, its kind. **Reads** what a read may show. **Stream** whether the answer stays open. **Preview** whether a screen preview may fetch it. **Audit** what a change writes first. **Assistants** the MCP tools that do the same.
