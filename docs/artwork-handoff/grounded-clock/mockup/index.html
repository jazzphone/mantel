Grounded clock: the Big Weather time

Mockup only: none of this is in the production screensaver. The sky and the meadow are the real screensaver's, captured in WebKit (Forecast, Layout: Big, the time as the big number). The numerals, their shadows and the grass in front are drawn live on top, from the same sun inputs hk-sky uses.

The grass in front of the bases is the stand-in that needs no new art: blade-shaped cut-outs of the meadow itself, so it matches every season and light by construction. The Codex artwork replaces it with detailed blades, seed heads and a few leaves. Guides show the ground line (orange), the camera horizon (blue), the tree line (green), the vanishing point, and where hk-sky draws the sun.
