How It Behaves

The plan is in docs/phase20/HOLIDAY-SLIDE.md.

  • Each holiday wears a sceneA scene is a set of painted pieces plus where its number goes: on the TV, on the tombstone, or standing on the meadow. Code places the pieces for each screen, so one set of art fits them all. Thanksgiving and New Year's are being painted next.
  • The same cornerTime, Date, Weather and House Status come from the slide corner, with its Time Size and its switches. The slide draws none of them.
  • The same turnsIt joins the slides among the photos (How Often: every N photos) and the additional screens over a clock (every N minutes).
  • Only when one is nearOutside its window, nothing shows and the other slides carry on. The first turn comes once a holiday is within the days you choose.
  • Effects stop when it leavesOne canvas, a fixed number of particles, 30 frames a second, and only while the slide is up. Reduce Motion or Effects Off draws one still picture.
  • The day itselfThe number gives way to the greeting, with more going on, until midnight. Then the next holiday's turn begins.
