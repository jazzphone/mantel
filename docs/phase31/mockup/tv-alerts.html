Notifications on the Apple TVs

Each picture below is what Mantel would draw for one notification: 1920 × 1080, drawn as SVG and turned into a JPEG on the server, then shown by HomeKit in the corner of every Apple TV. All three looks are offered, Color by default. Pick one; tap a picture to see it on the TV.

On the TV

The alert arrives over whatever is playing and leaves on its own after a few seconds.

How tvOS frames a camera alert (its size, corner and the name under it) is approximate here; the build's first step checks it on a real Apple TV.

Six of the house's notifications. The type is SF Pro and the glyphs are SF Symbols from the house's own files; this page stands in with your device's system font and hand-drawn glyphs.

In Settings

The default

Three looks, Color first

  • Color is HomeView's Color tiles: the accessory's own color, white on top. A light color is darkened until white type stays readable.
  • Black is the look of an iPhone's Live Activities and StandBy. Glass is the tablets' look: the house's background under dark glass.
  • The Apple TV page sets the house's look; any notification can choose its own on its page.

How Mantel draws it

  • A template per look, filled with the notification's title, its line, its room and time, its glyph and color.
  • SF Pro and SF Symbols from the house's files folder, the same ones the tablets draw with. Inter and Material glyphs where a house has none.
  • resvg turns the SVG into a JPEG in Mantel's image worker, in about a tenth of a second; the same card is drawn once.
  • A camera's notification (the doorbell) uses its own snapshot, full bleed, with the words on a dark fade at the bottom.
  • Type is sized for the corner: the title 168 px of 1080, two lines at most, names shortened by words.

Setting it up

  • Add to HomeKit has Mantel Link add the Mantel Alerts camera to Home Assistant's HomeKit Bridge. Nothing else changes in Home Assistant.
  • Scan the code in the Home app once. Settings says Paired when it is.
  • Phones get the same picture when this camera's notifications are on in the Home app; anyone who already gets Mantel's own notification can turn them off there.
