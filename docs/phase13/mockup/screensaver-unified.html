Living Room Tablet · Screensavermock-up, phase 13

Looks

A Look is the background and the time. Tapping one keeps everything you placed.

Pieces

One piece per fact, each in a few shapes. Tap one, then tap a green place on the screen.

One piece, every shape

The same renderer and the same units draw every shape. Size, Backdrop and Color work the same on each.

The slide corner

Each slide says what it already shows. The corner adds only the Time, Date, Weather and House Status it lacks, drawn by the pieces' renderer, with the time in the Look's face and color. These use the screen and the Look picked above.
